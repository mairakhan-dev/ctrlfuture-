FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend?
Any school or college student in Pakistan is welcome to attend. No prior experience required — just a passion to learn!
Is it online?
Yes! All sessions will be conducted live online via Google Meet. You can join from anywhere.
Will I receive a certificate?
Yes, all participants who complete the sessions will receive a digital certificate.
Can beginners join?
Absolutely! The sessions are designed for beginners and intermediate learners alike. All you need is curiosity.
What happens after registration?
You'll receive a confirmation email with session links, schedules, and all the details you need to get started.
Impact

Supporting The Citizens Foundation
